How this works

  1. Log every payment you receive, in USD or PKR.
  2. A share of it (5% by default) is set aside automatically at the live exchange rate.
  3. Send that balance to a cause whenever you’re ready, and track where it went.

Why 5%

A fixed, consistent share keeps giving simple to follow — no end-of-year math, no guessing what’s already been set aside.
